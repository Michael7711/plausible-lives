# Plausible Lives

An experimental project exploring AI-assisted, constraint-based
modelling of plausible human lives.

Developed through QLab at Queen's University Belfast.

## Research Direction

The project investigates whether AI can:

- Model the different things people might value in life.
- Generate large amounts of structured information.
- Construct different plausible life scenarios.
- Express life constraints using structured data.
- Identify and repair constraint violations.
- Compare plausible lives and interpret their differences as choices.

## Initial Research

The first stage focuses on understanding the Universal Automation
Wiki and its WorkSpec constraint-modelling system.

Reference: https://universalautomation.wiki/

## Development

This project is currently exploratory. The architecture and
implementation will evolve as the research progresses.
